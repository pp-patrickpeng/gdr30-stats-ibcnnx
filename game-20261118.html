ⓘHow we count
Unique connected points count each made shot or free throw once per player, even when several of that player’s actions led to it. Category totals can overlap. Teammates can both get credit for the same score, so player totals can overlap too. A steal, rebound or block links to the first team score in the next 4 logged plays and within 40 seconds of video. An opponent event, team turnover or missed shot ends the sequence. An assist tag must immediately precede a teammate’s made field goal, allowing consecutive assist tags. Repeated tags from the same passer count once. If multiple passers are tagged for the same basket, the assist is left unassigned. Points allowed off turnovers require an opponent score on the next logged play within 40 seconds. Shares use logged scoring. Glory League may omit events, especially for opponents, so these are estimates. Box-score stats are reported separately. For 18 Nov, assist tags immediately after a teammate basket within 6 seconds are matched, reflecting that game’s timeline order. Worked example
