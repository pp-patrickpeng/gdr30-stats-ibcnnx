Glossary

Every stat in plain words. Guides are rough for junior club basketball.

Box score basics

Points

Points scored.

2 × 2PM + 3 × 3PM + FTM

Read it next to how many shots it took.

Field goals (FG, FG%)

All shots from the floor, 2s and 3s. “6-11” means 6 made from 11 tried.

FGM ÷ FGA

Junior guide: 35–40% typical, 45%+ good.

2-pointers (2P%)

Shots inside the arc, including layups.

2PM ÷ 2PA

40–45% typical, 50%+ good.

3-pointers (3P%)

Shots from behind the arc.

3PM ÷ 3PA

33% from three is worth about the same as 50% on twos.

Free throws (FT%)

Unguarded 1-point shots after a foul.

FTM ÷ FTA

50–60% typical, 65%+ good.

Rebounds (OREB / DREB)

Offensive: our own miss, another try. Defensive: their miss, ends their turn.

REB = OREB + DREB

Team 8+ OREB is solid; DREB finishes a stop.

Assists

A pass that leads straight to a made basket.

count

Shows ball movement.

Steals & blocks

Steal: ball taken away. Block: shot deflected.

count

Team 8+ steals = an active defence.

Turnovers

Losing the ball without a shot: bad pass, travel, stolen.

count

Lower is better. Team target 8 or fewer.

Fouls

Personal fouls. 5 and you’re out.

count

Player 4+ = foul trouble.

Efficiency

True shooting (TS%)

Points per scoring attempt, counting 2s, 3s and free throws.

PTS ÷ (2 × (FGA + 0.44 × FTA))

42–48% typical, 50%+ good.

Effective FG% (eFG%)

FG% where a made three counts as 1.5 shots.

(FGM + 0.5 × 3PM) ÷ FGA

50%+ good.

Possessions

One turn with the ball. Estimated, as there’s no play-by-play.

FGA + 0.44 × FTA − OREB + TO

Lets us compare games with different pace.

Points per possession (PPP)

The truest measure of offence. Uses box-score points.

PTS ÷ possessions

0.75–0.90 typical, 1.00+ excellent.

Opponent PPP

Their official score per possession (using our possession count).

Opp PTS ÷ possessions

Under 0.70 is very strong defence.

Assists per turnover

How well we share the ball versus give it away.

AST ÷ TO

1.0+ good, 1.5+ very good.

Court words

Box-out

Turning and making contact to keep your opponent away from the rebound.

Crash the glass

Going hard to the rim for the offensive rebound when a teammate shoots.

Jump stop

Landing on two feet under control, so you can pivot, pass or shoot.

Extra pass

Passing up a good shot to find a teammate with a better one.

Verticality

Jumping straight up with arms up when defending a shot.

Outlet pass

The quick first pass after a defensive rebound to start the break.
