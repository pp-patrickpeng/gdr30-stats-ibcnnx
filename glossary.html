Basketball words used in the tips

Box-outTurning and making contact to keep your opponent away from the rebound.
Crash the glassGoing hard to the rim for the offensive rebound when a teammate shoots.
Jump stopLanding on two feet under control, so you can pivot, pass or shoot.
Reach-in foulSwiping at the ball instead of moving your feet.
VerticalityJumping straight up with arms up when defending a shot.
Extra passPassing up a good shot to find a teammate with a better one.
Shot fakePretending to shoot so the defender jumps or runs past you.
Outlet passThe quick first pass after a defensive rebound to start the break.
