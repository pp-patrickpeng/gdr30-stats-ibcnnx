How we count

This game's video tags. A made field goal is assisted when a teammate has an assist tag up to 4 seconds earlier. For 18 Nov, the new timeline records assists after the basket, so the immediately following assist tag within 6 seconds is matched instead. Without a matching tag it is counted as self-created.
